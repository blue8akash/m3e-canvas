## 🎨 Design System & UX Highlights

1. **Aesthetic Direction**:
   - **Modern Spiritual Minimalism**: Combines clean iOS/Android mobile ergonomics with sacred motifs and calm negative space.
   - **Color Harmony**: Deep meditative slate/indigo for dark theme, warm off-white linen tones for light theme, with saffron / marigold gold accents for spiritual focus.
2. **Key Product Pillars**:
   - **Personal Sadhana**: Daily Timer (Meditation/Japa), Reflection Journaling, Daily Insights.
   - **Progress & Consistency**: Sankalp (intentions), Streaks, Analytics, and 21/40-Day Challenges.
   - **Community & Service**: Sangha (discussion circles), Seva Board (volunteering), and Conscious Posts.
   - **Exploration & Education**: Yog Shala (asana guides), Audio Stories, Events, and Sacred Spaces map locator.
